# Humanizer

Remove AI-sounding patterns from text. Use when a draft reads like it was written by a chatbot and shouldn't.

## Kill these patterns

- "It's important to note that", "It goes without saying", "In today's fast-paced world".
- Rule-of-three lists everywhere ("innovative, efficient, and scalable").
- Em-dash overuse, and sentences that all have the same rhythm.
- Restating the question before answering it.
- Trailing summary paragraphs that repeat what was just said.

## Replace with

- Concrete nouns and verbs. "Cut latency 40%" beats "significantly improved performance".
- Varied sentence length. Short sentences hit hard. Then a longer one carries the nuance.
- Specific numbers, dates, names. Vague intensifiers ("very", "extremely") are noise.
- The reader's vocabulary, not the writer's comfort zone.

## Process

1. Read the draft aloud. Anything you wouldn't say to a colleague, cut.
2. Delete the first paragraph. It's usually throat-clearing.
3. Replace every adjective you can't defend with a number.
4. Keep exactly one idea per paragraph.
